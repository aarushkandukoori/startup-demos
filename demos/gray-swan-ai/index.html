The detector logic is real and runs in this page; the per-event milliseconds are a cost model (Stage A is length-proportional, Stage B adds a decode and normalisation pass). The admission controller checks the rolling p99 over the last 100 events, so roughly one event in a hundred is still allowed to exceed the budget — that is what a p99 target means.
