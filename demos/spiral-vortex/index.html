    How many times an encoding may hand its own buffers to another encoding.

    The compressor decides from eight evenly spaced chunks totalling this many rows, then applies the winner to the whole column.

    Candidate encodings

    Flat bit-packing, constant and validity encodings are always available, so the tree always has a leaf to fall back to.

    Baseline

    The baseline writes plain or dictionary pages and compresses each page as one LZ77 block, so a lookup must inflate a whole page.
